Clausent Styles:

Clausent — Design Style Comparison

8 visual directions for the contract intelligence platform. Each shows Site (landing page) and Dashboard (app) components.

1 Light Neobrutalism

Bold borders, solid shadows, playful-professional hybrid

Ref: Gumroad, Figma

2 Clean Minimal

Ultra-clean, invisible borders, generous spacing

Ref: Linear, Vercel, Raycast

3 Modern Enterprise

Gradients, strong hierarchy, enterprise trust signals

Ref: Stripe, HubSpot, Ironclad

4 Purpose-Driven Trust RECOMMENDED

Clean + trust hybrid, dark hero, teal glow accents

Ref: PandaDoc, DocuSign

5 Dark Mode Premium

Dark background, minimal, high contrast teal accents

Ref: Linear, Vercel, Pitch

6 AI-Native Aurora

Aurora gradients, glow effects, glassmorphism, futuristic

Ref: Jasper, Runway, Copy.ai

7 Glassmorphism

Translucent layers, backdrop blur, colorful shapes behind

Ref: Apple, Spotify

8 Soft Rounded SaaS

Friendly, extra-rounded, pastel tones, accessible feel

Ref: Notion, Asana, Dropbox
